# Digitized model from Duan et al. (2010)

Taken from:

Duan, Xueyang, Mahta Moghaddam, Daniel Wenkert, Rolando L. Jordan, and Suzanne E. Smrekar.
"X Band Model of Venus Atmosphere Permittivity."
Radio Science 45, no. 2 (2010).
<https://doi.org/10.1029/2009RS004169>.

## Digitized tables and figures

- Table 4: Concentration Versus Density for Sulfuric Acid
- Figure 6: Standard profiles of (a) electron density and (b) water vapor
- Figure 7: Standard profiles for (a) SO2, (b) H2SO4, and (d) CO
- Figure 8: Standard profiles for the mass content and concentration of cloud
- Figure 9: Relative permittivity profiles resulting from the standard atmospheric profiles.

## Annotations

1. Fig. 6: Implemented as a function and not digitized values.
2. Fig. 7: I only digitized the blue or red lines.
3. Table 4 is actually taken from: Lide, D. R. (Ed.) (2000),
   Handbook of Chemistry and Physics, 81st ed., CRC Press, Boca Raton, Fla.
