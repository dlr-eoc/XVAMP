## Annotations

Table 4-6 and 4-7 are digitized with the altitude column, since it is the same are the
respective values in Table 4-4 and 4-5.

Molecular oxygen concentrations may be estimated assuming O2/CO2 ~ 3 x 10^-3 (Fox, 1982).
