## Annotations

1. Altitudes are referenced to a planetary radius of 6,052 km.
2. The atmospheric mean molecular weight = 43.44.
3. The gas constant, R, = 191.4 j/kg K.
4. At the mean planet radius of 6,051.5 km (Pettengill et al., 1980), p = 95.0 bars and T = 737 K.
5. Γ, the adiabatic lapse rate, is taken from Seiff et al., 1980.
6. ς = p/(ρRT) is the imperfect gas compressibility factor, and is taken from Hilsenrath et al. (1960) for pure CO2.
